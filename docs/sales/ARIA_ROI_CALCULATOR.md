# Aria ROI calculator

Use this as a discussion worksheet, not as proven savings.

## Inputs

- matters per month
- average documents per matter
- average follow-up reminders per matter
- average draft preparation time today
- average review coordination time today

## Conservative assumptions

- document processing assistance: 5 minutes saved per supported document
- reminder and follow-up coordination: 2 minutes saved per reminder
- draft preparation support: 10 to 20 minutes saved per supported draft section
- client confirmation workflow: 5 to 10 minutes saved per matter

## Example formula

Estimated monthly hours saved =

`(supported_documents * 0.08) + (draft_actions * 0.2) + (confirmations * 0.06) + (pathway_runs * 0.15) + (reminders * 0.03)`

## Warning

Do not present this as audited ROI. Use it as a beta conversation tool and replace assumptions with real usage data once pilot workspaces are active.
