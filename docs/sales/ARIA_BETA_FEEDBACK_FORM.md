## Workflow questions

1. Which matters did you try in Aria?
2. Which workflows felt strongest?
3. Which workflows felt incomplete?
4. Where did the product save the most time?
5. Where did you still need manual work?
6. Which reminders, portal flows, or draft views felt most valuable?
7. Which safety warnings or review markers were unclear?

## Ratings

- setup experience: 1 to 5

- client portal usefulness: 1 to 5
- trust/confidence for beta use: 1 to 5
- likelihood to continue pilot: 1 to 5
