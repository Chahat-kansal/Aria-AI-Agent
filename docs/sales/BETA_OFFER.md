# Beta offer

## Structure

- up to 5 migration agents or firms in the first wave
- 30-day controlled beta
- dummy or sandbox matters first
- real client data only after legal, privacy, and security review
- discounted founding beta pricing
- feedback required

## What Aria is for

- evidence collection and review
- client portal workflow
- draft preparation support
- deadline and follow-up visibility
- safe review-first automation

## What Aria is not claiming

- final legal advice
- guaranteed visa outcomes
- auto-lodgement
- fully automated support for every subclass
