# Aria limitations and safety

## Required positioning

- Aria is AI-assisted workflow software.
- Aria helps migration professionals prepare and review cases.
- Aria does not provide final legal advice.
- Aria does not guarantee visa outcomes.
- Aria does not auto-lodge applications.
- Aria outputs remain ready for agent final review.

## Current known limitations

- Some subclasses are fully supported, while others are checklist-first or scaffold-only.
- Official form mapping coverage is still partial.
- Photo OCR must not be claimed unless a live OCR provider is configured.
- Generated drafts still require migration-agent review and client confirmation where applicable.
- Platform and production posture should still be independently reviewed for legal, privacy, and security readiness.

## Demo safety rules

- Use only dummy data.
- Do not expose raw portal tokens.
- Do not expose token hashes.
- Do not expose raw document URLs.
- Do not present scaffold workflows as complete automation.
