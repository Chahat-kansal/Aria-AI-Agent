# Aria beta pricing

This is a suggested pricing model for early beta conversations. It is not proof of revenue and does not imply existing paying customers.

## Suggested beta pricing

- Founding beta: AUD $149 to $249 per month
- Early agency: AUD $299 per month
- Pro target after stronger proof: AUD $399 per month
- Team or firm tier: later, once multi-user operational proof is stronger

## Suggested beta terms

- 30-day controlled beta
- Dummy or sandbox matters first
- Real client data only after legal, privacy, and security review
- Feedback required
- No guarantee of outcome
- No final legal advice

## Suggested beta limits

- limited number of active staff users
- limited number of matters
- direct product feedback channel
- optional white-glove onboarding
